# Portfolio

My personal portfolio website, built from scratch to showcase my most important academic, research, engineering, and software experience.

The portfolio focuses on my work in **computer engineering, embedded systems, hardware, software, XR/VR, and cybersecurity**. I wanted it to be more than a traditional resume, so I designed it as an interactive space where projects, research, and experience can be explored in more detail.

## Built With

* **HTML** for the structure and content
* **CSS** for the layout, styling, animations, and visual design
* **JavaScript** for interactive elements and website functionality
* **GitHub Pages** for hosting and deployment

## Design

The website was designed and developed from scratch. I created the overall concept, layout, navigation, typography, terminal-style interface, and visual elements myself using **Figma**.

The design is inspired by engineering notebooks, technical documentation, and development environments. I wanted the interface to feel technical and organized while still being easy to navigate.

I also used **AI agents as development assistants** to help with implementation, debugging, accessibility, and exploring different technical solutions. The concept, content, structure, and design decisions were created by me.

## Portfolio Structure

The website includes the most important parts of my experience:

* **Projects**: Software, hardware, embedded systems, and engineering projects
* **Research**: Research projects and publications, including work related to XR and motion data privacy
* **Involvement**: Leadership, student organizations, volunteering, and other activities
* **Resume**: Education, technical skills, experience, research, and projects
* **Contact**: Contact information and links

## Purpose

I built this portfolio to bring my most relevant experience into one place and make it easier for recruiters, researchers, professors, and other engineers to understand my background and technical interests.

Instead of listing every experience, the portfolio focuses on the projects, research, and activities that best represent my skills and current direction.
