Taking Metaverse Risks Into Real-Life: User Identification Based On Their Virtual Avatars

Experimental Undergraduate Research

Experimental study of privacy risks from motion data in VR. Participants are recorded from side-view perspectives resembling street and security cameras while performing movements in VR, to investigate whether individuals can be recognized or re-identified from their motion alone. The study is currently in development, and its methodology and final research terminology have not yet been finalized.
