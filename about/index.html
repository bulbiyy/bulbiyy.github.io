Hi! My name is Alina...

I am a 22-year-old international Computer Engineering junior and Honors student at the University of Florida. Originally from Belarus, I studied at UWC Adriatic in Italy, where I completed the International Baccalaureate in a community of students from around the world. Living in different countries and cultures from an early age shaped my curiosity, adaptability, and interest in technology and people.

My interests sit at the intersection of hardware and software. I enjoy building with microcontrollers, embedded systems, sensors, and other physical devices, while also exploring programming, data, and cybersecurity. Recently, I have become particularly interested in XR and VR technologies, sensing, and the security challenges that come with collecting and using new types of data.

I am currently involved in research on motion data privacy in extended reality, alongside engineering projects involving embedded systems and hardware-software integration. I am looking for research and internship opportunities where I can explore the intersection of XR, VR, sensing, cybersecurity, and embedded technology while continuing to build practical engineering experience.

Outside of engineering, I have traveled extensively across Europe through volunteering and personal exploration, experiences that have kept me curious about different cultures and perspectives. I also enjoy being involved in my university community and currently serve as President of the Russian Club, while participating in Audio Design & Engineering and the Ukrainian Rebuilding Initiative.
