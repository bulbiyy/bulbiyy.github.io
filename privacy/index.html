Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

What this site is

This is a personal portfolio for Alina Vasina. It is a static website: there is no account system, no login, and no database of visitors.

What is collected

This site does not set cookies, does not run analytics, and does not build a profile of you. Nothing you do here is stored on the site itself, with one exception: your browser remembers whether you left the side menu open or closed, using local storage on your own device. That preference never leaves your computer and you can clear it by clearing your browser data.

Hosting and server logs

Like any website, the service hosting these files may keep standard technical logs — IP address, browser type, time of request — for security and reliability. Those logs are handled by the hosting provider under their own policy, not by this site.

Fonts and external links

  • Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page loads. That request is subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Google Scholar and other sites take you off this site. Once you leave, their policies apply, not this one.

Email

If you email me, I have your message and your address. I use them to reply to you and nothing else. I do not add addresses to any list and do not pass them on.

Your rights

Because this site holds no personal data about you, there is nothing here to access, correct or delete. If you have emailed me and want that correspondence deleted, ask and I will remove it.
